Prevention Tips

Protect Your Skin Health

Evidence-based tips to reduce your risk and maintain healthy skin year-round.

Use SPF 30+ Daily

Apply broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning, even on cloudy days. Reapply every 2 hours outdoors.

Monthly Self-Checks

Examine your skin monthly using the ABCDE rule: Asymmetry, Border, Color, Diameter, Evolution.

Annual Dermatologist Visit

Schedule a full-body skin exam every year with a board-certified dermatologist.

Stay Hydrated

Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily. Well-hydrated skin is healthier and more resilient.

Antioxidant-Rich Diet

Eat berries, leafy greens, and foods rich in vitamins C and E to protect skin from UV damage.

Avoid Tanning Beds

Indoor tanning increases melanoma risk by 75%. Opt for self-tanning products instead.
